Northwind Network Knowledge Base
Document: Office Wi-Fi, Guest Network, and Wired Access
Audience: All employees
Owner: Network Operations

1. Corporate SSID
Connect only to NORTHWIND-CORP. The guest SSID NORTHWIND-GUEST has no access to file shares, printers, or intranet.

2. First-time Wi-Fi
Use your company email and network password. The profile is pushed by Intune. If you are prompted for a certificate, choose Northwind Device Cert.

3. Slow or dropping Wi-Fi
Forget the network, reboot the laptop, reconnect.
Move closer to an access point. Conference rooms on floor 4 are known dead zones; use a docking station Ethernet port there.
If many people in one area are affected, this is an access-point outage. Escalate as HIGH.

4. Ethernet
Docking stations on NORTHWIND-CORP provide 1 Gbps. Do not plug personal switches into wall ports.

5. DNS
Internal names such as intranet.northwind.internal resolve only on corp Wi-Fi or VPN. If they fail at home without VPN, this is expected, not an incident.
