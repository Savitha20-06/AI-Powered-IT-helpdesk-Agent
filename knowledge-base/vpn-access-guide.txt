Northwind Corporate IT Knowledge Base
Document: VPN Access Policy and Setup Guide
Audience: All employees
Owner: Network Operations

1. Purpose
Employees may connect to internal systems only through the approved corporate VPN. Personal VPN tools are not permitted on company laptops.

2. Approved client
Install Cisco Secure Client (AnyConnect) from the Company Software Center. Do not download VPN software from the public internet.

3. How to connect
Step 1: Connect to a trusted internet network.
Step 2: Open Cisco Secure Client.
Step 3: Enter gateway vpn.northwind.internal
Step 4: Sign in with your company email and network password.
Step 5: Approve the Microsoft Authenticator MFA prompt.
Step 6: Wait until the status shows Connected. The office 10.20.0.0/16 network then becomes reachable.

4. Common errors
- "Login failed": reset your network password in the Identity Portal, then retry.
- "Certificate untrusted": your laptop is missing the Northwind Root CA. Raise an IT ticket. Do not ignore certificate warnings.
- "MFA timeout": open Authenticator before clicking Connect.
- Split tunnel is disabled. All traffic goes through VPN while connected.

5. Disconnect policy
Disconnect when you leave the laptop unattended. Overnight VPN sessions are automatically dropped after 12 hours.

6. Support
If the gateway is unreachable after a password reset and a laptop reboot, escalate to Network Operations with your public IP and the exact error screenshot.
