Northwind Workplace Knowledge Base
Document: Printer Setup and Jam Recovery
Audience: Office employees
Owner: End User Computing

1. Approved printers
Floor printers are named NW-PRN-FLOOR{n}. Home printers are not supported on company laptops.

2. Install a floor printer
Open Company Software Center > Printers.
Select the printer for your floor.
The driver is already signed. Do not download HP or Canon drivers from the vendor website.

3. Paper jam
Open the front panel, remove trapped paper in the direction of the path arrows, close all trays fully, and print a test page from the printer LCD.
If the jam repeats three times, stop using the device and place an IT ticket with the printer name from the LCD.

4. Access denied
Printers use badge-based secure print. Hold your badge on the reader, then select your job.
If badge print fails, your card may not be mapped. Escalate to IT with your employee number. Do not borrow another person's badge.

5. Toner
Only Facilities may replace toner. Empty cartridges go to the recycling bin in the copy room.
