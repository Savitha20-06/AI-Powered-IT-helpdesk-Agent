Northwind Identity Knowledge Base
Document: Password Reset, Account Lockout, and MFA
Audience: All employees
Owner: Identity and Access Management

1. Password rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters, include upper, lower, number, and symbol, and cannot be reused from the last 12 passwords. Passwords expire every 90 days.

2. Self-service reset
Go to https://identity.northwind.internal/reset
Verify with company email plus Authenticator code.
Set a new password. Wait 2 minutes for Active Directory replication before unlocking Outlook or VPN.

3. Account lockout
Five failed password attempts lock the account for 15 minutes. Do not keep retrying. Use self-service reset or contact the service desk.

4. MFA lost phone
If you lose your phone, do not ask a colleague to approve MFA.
IT Staff can issue a temporary 8-hour bypass after verifying employee ID in person or over a recorded video call.
A new Authenticator enrollment must be completed within those 8 hours.

5. Shared passwords
Sharing passwords or writing them on sticky notes is a policy violation. Use the approved password manager only.

6. After a reset
Reconnect VPN, lock and unlock Windows, then open Outlook. If mailbox still asks for the old password, sign out of Office completely and sign in again.
